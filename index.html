How to use Cash Flow Studio

START HERE

A practical guide to the offline educational demo. Use fictional information only; this is not a bank account or tax filing service.

1. Start with the overview

Open Overview and select a month. Income and expenses reflect transactions recorded for that month. Upcoming bills include unpaid bills due that month.

Money available = income − recorded expenses − unpaid bills − planned monthly savings set-asides. It is a planning indicator, not a verified bank balance.

2. Add transactions

Open Transactions, select + Add transaction, then enter a date, type, category, description and positive amount. Income increases net cash flow; Expense decreases it; Transfer is tracked without reducing net cash flow.

3. Manage bills without double counting

Open Bills calendar and select + Add bill. When the bill is actually paid, select Unpaid and confirm: the app marks it paid and creates one linked expense automatically.

To reverse it, select Paid and confirm removal of the linked expense. Do not also add the same payment manually. If you already entered the expense, cancel the automatic payment action to avoid double counting.

Legacy paid bills may not have linked transactions. Deleting a paid bill preserves its linked expense as an ordinary transaction.

4. Set savings goals

Open Savings goals, add a target, an amount already saved and a monthly set-aside. The set-aside affects the available-money planning estimate but is not automatically recorded as an expense.

5. Explore the Tax Center

Enter fictional US business revenue, business expenses and a reserve percentage. The calculator applies that percentage to nonnegative business profit. It does not calculate tax owed, refunds or filing eligibility. Do not upload real W-2, 1099 or identity documents.

6. Customize and back up

In Settings, choose display language and currency. Changing currency does not convert amounts. Export a demo JSON backup before importing another: import replaces existing demo records after confirmation. Restore sample data or clear local data only when you intend to replace or remove them.

Privacy, offline use and limitations

Records are stored in this browser profile, not synchronized to a bank or cloud account. Other people using the same browser profile may access them. An installed PWA may work offline after its resources are cached successfully, but offline operation must be tested on your device. Clearing browser storage can permanently erase locally stored demo entries; keep an exported backup.

For testing only: never enter real account numbers, passwords, tax IDs, medical information or confidential financial records.

Preferences & local demo controls

Display preferences

No exchange conversion: changing the currency changes only the symbol and formatting. Existing amounts are NOT converted. Use a single currency for all entries; never mix USD and CAD in the same dataset.

Tax Center is a US-only educational scenario, regardless of display currency. No Canadian or other national tax rules are implemented.

Demo data is stored only in this browser's localStorage. Anyone with access to this browser profile may be able to read it. Do not enter real financial or tax information.

Backup imports require confirmation and replace all current demo records. Export your current data first. No accounts, bank integrations, automatic backups, or cloud synchronization.
